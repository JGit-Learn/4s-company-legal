Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

The short version

  • 4's Company is a double-dating app. You join with a friend as a pair, and your pair meets other pairs. Your profile is always shown as part of your pair's joint profile.
  • We collect what the app needs to work: your account details, the profile you build with your partner, your photos, your messages, and the safety tools you use (reports, blocks and verification).
  • Other pairs see your joint profile, including your first name, age, photos and prompts. They never see your email, your exact birthday or your messages with other pairs.
  • Photos are checked for safety before they go live, automatically by Google Cloud Vision and sometimes by a person on our team.
  • Selfie verification is reviewed by a person and the selfie is deleted once it's reviewed.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time (Profile → Account → Delete Account).

6. Photo moderation, verification and safety

Photo checks

Every photo you upload is held privately until it's checked. We send it to Google Cloud Vision, which returns likelihood scores for adult, racy, violent, medical and spoof content. Depending on the scores, a photo is published, rejected, or held for a person on our team to review. Rejected photos are deleted. If an identical photo was already checked, we reuse that decision instead of scanning it again.

Selfie verification (optional)

If you choose to verify, your selfie is uploaded privately and a reviewer on our team compares it, by eye, with your main solo photo. We don't create a face template or other biometric identifier. Your selfie is deleted as soon as the reviewer approves or rejects it. Your verified status is tied to that solo photo: if you replace it, or it's removed, your badge expires and you can verify again.

Reports and enforcement

When you report a profile, photo or message, we keep a copy of what was reported (for messages, the text and the conversation it was part of) so we can review it even if the other pair unmatches, blocks you, or deletes their account. If a pair is reported by several different members, it may be hidden from Discover until a person reviews it. Our team can suspend or ban accounts; every action they take is recorded in an internal audit log.

8. How long we keep your data

  • While your account is open, we keep your data so the Service works.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your account, profile, pair, photos, messages, push tokens and pending verification requests.
  • Verification selfies are deleted as soon as they're reviewed. A selfie you replace before review is deleted once your next one is reviewed.
  • Safety records are kept longer: reports (including copies of reported content), moderation decisions and our internal audit log. We keep them to protect the community, prevent banned members from returning, handle appeals, and meet legal obligations. They're kept for [retention period, e.g. 3 years], after which they're deleted or de-identified.
  • Technical logs are kept for a limited time by our hosting providers under their own retention settings.

9. How we protect your data

We use industry-standard protections, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS).
  • Database rules that let each member access only the data they're allowed to see.
  • Private storage for unreviewed photos and selfies.
  • Hashed passwords.
  • A moderation dashboard that requires two-factor authentication, with every action logged.

No system is perfectly secure. Please use a strong, unique password, and tell us right away if you think your account has been compromised.

10. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in the United States. If you use the Service from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S., where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

11. Children's privacy

The Service is only for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and our systems reject birthdays under 18. If you believe someone under 18 is using the Service, please report them in the app or contact us, and we'll remove the account.
